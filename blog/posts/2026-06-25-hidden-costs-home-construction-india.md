Every first-time builder in India underestimates total project cost by 15-25%. Not because they pick the wrong cement or steel — those numbers are easy to look up. They underestimate because of the **hidden, non-line-item costs nobody mentions until they hit you**. This post lists them, with Chennai 2026 numbers, so you can budget honestly.

## 1. Mason / labour advances and "festival money"

The contractor quotes you ₹3 lakh for labour. What's not in the quote:

- **Mason advance** — you'll pay 30-40% upfront before work starts (₹90,000 – ₹1.2 lakh held for the duration)
- **Diwali / Pongal bonus** — 10-15 days of wages as bonus during festivals if your project crosses the festival (₹15,000 – ₹40,000 typical)
- **Weather idle days** — rain days when masons can't work but still need to be paid daily wage (Chennai monsoon adds 8-12 days; typical mason pay ₹800-1,000/day × 4 masons = ₹3,000-4,000/day × 10 days = ₹30,000-40,000)
- **Idle days from your side** — material shortage, design indecision, plan revisions (each idle day costs ₹3,000-5,000)

**Hidden cost: ₹1.5 – 2.5 lakh** over a typical 6-month build.

## 2. Watchman / chowkidar

You can't leave a Chennai construction site unguarded — material theft (especially TMT steel and cement) is real. Options:

- Hire a watchman: ₹12,000-18,000/month × 6 months = **₹72,000 – ₹1.08 lakh**
- Pay a neighbour or trusted local to keep an eye: ₹3,000-5,000/month × 6 = ₹18,000-30,000 (less reliable)
- CCTV + remote monitoring: one-time ₹15,000-25,000 + ₹3,000-5,000/month internet = ₹30,000-50,000 over project

**Hidden cost: ₹30,000 – ₹1 lakh** depending on choice.

## 3. Temporary connections — electricity and water

Before your permanent EB connection comes through, you need power for the site. Same for water.

- **Temporary EB connection** (TNEB): ₹8,000-15,000 deposit + monthly meter charges + bill
- **Temporary borewell or tanker water**: ₹3,000-8,000/month for tanker supply if no borewell on site
- **Permanent EB connection** (post-construction): ₹10,000-25,000 service connection charge + cable laying
- **CMWSSB water + sewerage**: ₹15,000-35,000 deposit + connection charges

**Hidden cost: ₹40,000 – ₹80,000**.

## 4. Stamp duty + registration on the construction agreement

If you sign a construction agreement with a contractor (you should, for legal protection), it attracts **stamp duty + registration**:

- **Stamp duty**: ~1% of construction value in Tamil Nadu (₹22,000 on a ₹22-lakh build)
- **Registration fee**: ~1% (another ₹22,000)
- **Lawyer/document drafting**: ₹5,000-15,000

**Hidden cost: ₹40,000 – ₹60,000**.

For owner-built projects (no contractor agreement) this is zero, but most middle-income builders sign a contract.

## 5. Architect / engineer fees beyond initial design

The architect quotes ₹40,000 for "design + plan + permit assistance". What's typically extra:

- **Site visits during construction** (₹2,000-5,000/visit × 6-10 visits = ₹15,000-50,000)
- **Revised drawings if you change something** mid-build (₹5,000-15,000 per revision)
- **Structural engineer separate fee** if not bundled (₹15,000-30,000)
- **PMC (Project Management Consultant)** if you hire one to oversee daily work (5-7% of construction cost — i.e., ₹1.1-1.5 lakh on a ₹22-lakh build)

**Hidden cost: ₹50,000 – ₹2 lakh** depending on involvement level.

## 6. CMDA / DTCP / local body charges beyond approval

You expect to pay ₹40,000-80,000 for the [CMDA plan approval](/blog/plan-approval-and-cmda-permits-in-chennai-the-2026-process-explained/). What's often missed:

- **Service tax / GST on government services** (~5-10%)
- **Liaison / runner fees** (the person who hand-carries documents to CMDA / corporation offices, ₹5,000-15,000)
- **Re-submission fees** for objections (₹3,000-7,500 per cycle, often 2-3 cycles)
- **Building Plan Approval (BPA) gratuity** — informal facilitation payments, illegal but commonly paid (varies wildly)

**Hidden cost: ₹15,000 – ₹50,000+ depending on local practices**.

## 7. Soil testing

Mandatory for any G+1 or above, also recommended for coastal builds. Often forgotten:

- **3-borehole soil test**: ₹15,000-25,000
- **Detailed soil report with foundation recommendation**: included if engaged a proper soil consultant

**Hidden cost: ₹15,000 – ₹25,000**. Skipping this is risky — bad soil assumption can cost ₹1-3 lakh in foundation redesign.

## 8. Tool and equipment hire

You're not paying for the cement mixer — but you're paying for:

- **Mixer hire** (if not owned by mason): ₹300-500/day × 30-40 days = ₹10,000-20,000
- **Vibrator** for concrete: ₹400-600/day during slab pours
- **Scaffolding** (steel/aluminium tubes + planks): ₹15,000-30,000 hire over project, or buy used for ₹40,000-60,000
- **Welding sets, drilling, cutting tools** — usually included in labour but sometimes extra
- **Hoist or chain pulley** for upper floors: ₹500-1,000/day during upper-floor work
- **Pump truck or boom pump** for slab pours on upper floors: ₹8,000-15,000 per pour

**Hidden cost: ₹30,000 – ₹70,000**.

## 9. Transportation and unloading

The cement bag is ₹420 delivered — but for self-purchased materials from a hardware shop:

- **Truck/tipper hire** to bring sand, jelly, blocks from the supplier to your site: ₹1,500-4,000 per trip (often 5-10 trips over the project)
- **Hand-unloading labour** at site for cement bags, TMT bundles, blocks: ₹500-1,500 per delivery
- **Storage shed / temporary cover** for cement bags: ₹3,000-8,000

## 10. Wastage and "extra coverage"

Quoted material quantities assume 5% wastage. Reality is often 8-12% because of:

- Mason mistakes (over-cuts, over-mixing)
- Broken tiles, chipped blocks, dropped paint
- Wrong colour ordered, has to be redone
- Theft from site (small quantities of paint, fittings, fasteners)

**Hidden cost: 3-5% over the material budget — ₹40,000-70,000** on a ₹15-lakh material spend.

## 11. Furniture, fittings, and "scope creep"

You start with "I'll buy a basic ceiling fan" — by handover, you've spent:

- Ceiling fans (4-6 × ₹3,000-8,000 each) = ₹12,000-48,000
- Geysers / water heaters (2-3 × ₹6,000-15,000) = ₹12,000-45,000
- Inverter/UPS for power cuts (₹15,000-40,000)
- Modular wardrobe / kitchen upgrades (₹30,000-1.5 lakh beyond initial scope)
- Curtains, blinds, rods (₹15,000-50,000)
- Initial furniture (sofa, beds, dining) — easily ₹2-5+ lakh
- Appliances (fridge, washing machine, AC, microwave) — ₹1-3+ lakh

**Hidden cost: ₹2 – 6+ lakh** — depending on what you call "construction" vs "moving in".

## 12. Vastu / pooja / housewarming

Cultural costs are real and almost always forgotten in the spreadsheet:

- **Bhoomi pooja** before construction starts: ₹5,000-15,000
- **Vastu consultant** if engaged: ₹5,000-25,000 (often gives plan revisions)
- **Foundation stone / pooja during work**: ₹3,000-10,000
- **Griha pravesh** ceremony after completion: ₹20,000-1 lakh

**Hidden cost: ₹30,000 – ₹1.5 lakh**.

## 13. Post-handover snag fixing

You think it's done. It isn't. Within the first 2-6 months:

- **Plumbing leaks** — joint corrections, sometimes whole-pipe replacement
- **Electrical issues** — flickering lights, MCB tripping, weak earthing
- **Paint touch-ups** — masons drag furniture during move-in, scuffs everywhere
- **Door alignment** — wood swelling in monsoon means doors stick or don't close
- **Tile lifting** — bad adhesive batch or moisture leads to hollow tiles

**Hidden cost: ₹15,000 – ₹50,000** in the first year of occupancy.

## 14. Insurance — usually not bought, often regretted

Construction sites should be insured, most aren't. Options:

- **Contractor's All Risk (CAR) policy**: covers fire, theft, third-party liability during construction. ~0.3-0.5% of project value, so ₹6,000-12,000 on a ₹22-lakh build. Cheap insurance against catastrophic loss.
- **Workmen Compensation** insurance for mason injuries: ₹5,000-10,000/year. Legally required if you employ workers directly.
- **Home insurance** post-handover: ₹3,000-8,000/year

Many builders skip this and roll the dice. One site fire or worker injury can cost ₹5-50 lakh out of pocket.

**Hidden cost (if bought): ₹15,000 – ₹30,000. If not bought: 0 — but huge tail risk.**

## How to budget honestly

1. **Take your visible construction estimate** (materials + labour + design + permits, e.g., ₹22 lakh)
2. **Add 25-35% as hidden costs**, depending on how comprehensive your initial quote was
3. **Add 10-15% on top as contingency** for unknowns
4. **Result**: realistic total project budget

For a ₹22-lakh visible quote: budget **₹30-35 lakh** to be safe.
