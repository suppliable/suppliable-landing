## Setup — typical Chennai 3BHK

- **Carpet area**: 1,500 sqft
- **Wall surface area (interior, both sides counted once)**: ~3,200 sqft
- **External wall area** (single-floor apartment façade or independent house): ~1,800 sqft
- **Coats applied**: 2 interior, 2 exterior
- **Surface prep**: putty (2 coats) + primer (1 coat)

## Step 1 — Interior paint quantity

**Asian Paints Tractor Emulsion** at 140 sqft per litre per coat:

```
Litres = (3,200 sqft × 2 coats) ÷ 140 sqft/L
       = 6,400 ÷ 140
       = 45.7 L → order 50 L
```

Best pack combo for 50 L:
- 2 × 20 L buckets = 40 L
- 1 × 10 L can = 10 L

**Cost (Chennai, June 2026)**: 2 × ₹4,800 + 1 × ₹2,500 = **₹12,100** for paint alone.

## Step 2 — Exterior paint quantity

**Asian Paints Ace Exterior Emulsion** at 110 sqft per litre per coat:

```
Litres = (1,800 sqft × 2 coats) ÷ 110
       = 3,600 ÷ 110
       = 32.7 L → order 35 L
```

Best pack combo:
- 1 × 20 L bucket = 20 L
- 1 × 10 L can = 10 L
- 1 × 4 L = 4 L (slight over for touch-up)

**Cost**: ₹6,200 + ₹3,250 + ₹1,350 = **₹10,800**.

If your build is on ECR or any coastal location, upgrade to **Apex Ultima** (3× longer-lasting in salt air, ₹450-500/L vs Ace's ₹310): cost goes to ~₹15,500.

## Step 3 — Putty (wall smoothening)

**Birla White Wall Care Putty** coverage: ~12 sqft/kg per coat. Two coats over 3,200 sqft interior:

```
Putty kg = (3,200 × 2) ÷ 12 = 533 kg → order 540 kg
```

Pack: 40 kg bags × 14 bags. Chennai price: ~₹38/kg.

**Cost**: 540 × ₹38 = **₹20,520**.

If walls are already smooth (skim coat only): ~half this.

## Step 4 — Primer

**Asian Paints Primer (water-based)**: 1 coat over putty, coverage ~120 sqft/L:

```
Primer = 3,200 ÷ 120 = 26.7 L → order 30 L
```

Pack: 1 × 20 L + 1 × 10 L. Chennai price: ~₹230/L.

**Cost**: 30 × ₹230 = **₹6,900**.

(Exterior usually doesn't need separate primer — Ace bonds direct to plastered surface with one diluted first coat.)

## Step 5 — Labour

Chennai painting labour rates (June 2026):

| Job | Rate |
|---|---|
| Putty + 2 coats interior emulsion | ₹16-20/sqft of wall |
| 2 coats exterior emulsion | ₹14-18/sqft of wall |
| Premium finishes (texture, accent walls) | ₹30+/sqft |

For our 3,200 sqft interior + 1,800 sqft exterior:

- Interior: 3,200 × ₹18 = ₹57,600
- Exterior: 1,800 × ₹16 = ₹28,800

**Labour total**: **₹86,400** (round to ₹85,000).

Brushes, rollers, trays, sandpaper, masking tape, drop cloths, scaffolding hire:

**Cost**: **₹8,000 – 12,000** depending on whether painter brings their own kit.

## Where most people overspend

1. **Buying small packs**. 4 L cans cost ₹240/L; 20 L buckets cost ₹240/L too — but 1 L cans cost ₹290/L. Order the biggest pack you'll fully use.
2. **Premium brand for interior when nobody sees it**. Royale Aspira on bedroom walls is wasted money — Tractor at 1/3 the cost is indistinguishable to non-paint-experts and lasts the same 3-4 years before repaint.
3. **Skipping primer to save ₹7,000**. Without primer, putty absorbs the emulsion unevenly → patchy finish → 1 extra coat needed → costs ₹15,000 in extra paint + labour to fix.
4. **Hiring a generic painter for exterior work**. Exterior requires waterproofing knowledge — improper edge sealing on parapets causes leaks within a year.

## Timing — how long does it take?

For a 1500 sqft 3BHK with 2 painters:

- Day 1: Putty coat 1
- Day 2: Putty coat 2 + sanding
- Day 3: Primer coat (dries overnight)
- Day 4: First emulsion coat (interior)
- Day 5: Second emulsion coat (interior)
- Day 6: Exterior coat 1
- Day 7: Exterior coat 2 + touch-ups

**Total: 7 working days, single crew.** Double crew → 4 days.
