## Three quality tiers — what they actually mean

| Tier | ₹/sqft range | Total for 1000 sqft | Typical buyer |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Budget** | ₹1,600 – ₹2,000 | ₹16 – 20 lakh | First-time builder, smaller plot, basic finishes |
| **Standard** | ₹2,000 – ₹2,800 | ₹20 – 28 lakh | Most middle-class Chennai homes |
| **Premium** | ₹2,800 – ₹3,500+ | ₹28 – 35+ lakh | Bigger windows, vitrified tiles, modular kitchen, branded fittings |

These numbers are for a **G+0 (ground floor only)** house. G+1 doesn't double the cost — typically 1.7-1.8× because the foundation, slab, plumbing risers and electrical mains are shared.

## Standard 1000 sqft build — line by line (₹22-25 lakh total)

This is the most common Chennai middle-class build. RCC frame, AAC block walls, vitrified flooring, single-storey, two bedrooms, two bathrooms, one car park.

### A. Structure (₹6.5 – 7.5 lakh, ~30% of total)

| Item | Quantity | Chennai 2026 cost |
|---|---|---|
| Cement (UltraTech / Ramco PPC) | ~280 bags | ₹1,15,000 |
| TMT steel (Fe 550D, ARS) | ~3.5 tonnes | ₹2,17,000 |
| M-sand | ~12 tonnes | ₹17,000 |
| 20 mm jelly (aggregate) | ~20 tonnes | ₹26,000 |
| AAC blocks (Reenacon 6" + 4") | ~2,500 | ₹1,55,000 |
| Block Fix adhesive (Ramco) | ~22 bags | ₹8,000 |

Foundation type matters: open foundation on hard rocky soil saves ₹50,000-1 lakh vs raft on coastal soil east of OMR.

| Item | Cost |

## Where most overruns happen

- **Site preparation** if soil isn't what was assumed (₹50k – 2 lakh overrun)
- **Steel price changes** mid-build (₹50k – 1 lakh)
- **Sand availability** during the seasonal ban (₹30-50k)
- **Kitchen + bathroom upgrade after seeing showroom samples** (₹1-3 lakh)
- **Furniture and lighting "in scope creep"** post-handover

## What's excluded from this number

- **Land cost** — Chennai plot prices vary 10× by area
- **Compound wall + gate + landscaping**
- **Solar panels, water tank, motor, generator**
- **Interior furniture and decor**
- **Architect/PMC fees beyond basic design** if you hire a turnkey contractor with full PMC

## Realistic per-sqft benchmarks by area

In our experience supplying Chennai builds:

- **OMR / Sholinganallur / IT corridor**: ₹2,300 – ₹3,000/sqft (logistics easy, labour abundant)
- **ECR coastal builds**: ₹2,500 – ₹3,200/sqft (CRS steel premium, salt-air UPVC mandatory)
- **Velachery / Pallavaram / Tambaram**: ₹2,200 – ₹2,800/sqft (most cost-efficient)
- **Anna Nagar / T. Nagar / Mylapore**: ₹2,500 – ₹3,500/sqft (older area, narrow lanes, premium-finish expectations)
